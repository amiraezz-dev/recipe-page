Vanilla Cupcakes

This is a quick and easy vanilla cupcake recipe.

Ingredients

cupcakes

  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 0.25 cup milk

Icing

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup butter
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 0.25 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to medium heat.
  2. Line a 12-hole cupcake tin with 12 paper cases.
  3. Mix the butter and sugar in a bowl until fluffy.
  4. Add the eggs one after another while mixing.
  5. Add the vanilla extract and the flour while mixing
  6. Gradually add the milk
  7. Divide the mixture equally in the tin holes
  8. Bake for 25 minutes
  9. Take the cakes out of the tin after 5 minutes.
  10. Place them on a wire rack to cool
  11. Add half the icing sugar in a bowl with the butter and mix until fluffy
  12. While mixing, add the remaining icing sugar, vanilla and milk
  13. Spread the icing equally on the cupcakes
